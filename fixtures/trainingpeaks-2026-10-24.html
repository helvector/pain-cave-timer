Steady State intervals are ridden within your Threshold Zone, just below FTP. By accumulating significant time at an intensity range just below FTP, Steady State intervals apply a training stimulus to ultimately increase your FTP.

Intensity Ranges:
RPE: 8-9
Heart Rate: 95-100% of THR
Power: 96-100% of FTP
Cadence: 80-95 RPM

  1. 1

    Warm up

    10 min @ 143-195 W - Recovery-Endurance
  2. 2

    Repeat 4 times

    • Steady State 8 min @ 250-260 W - Threshold - 80-95 rpm
    • Recovery 4 min @ 3-140 W - Recovery
  3. 3

    Cool Down

    12 min @ 104-140 W - Recovery
