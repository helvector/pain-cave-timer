Workout

Cycling Specific Weight Work

Sets

20

Exercises

8

Should be performed 2-3 times per week to make adaptations & receive longterm benefits. Goal & focus for each exercise is not the amount of weight (or load), but fluidity & form during exercise. You are not trying to gain muscle mass as a cyclist. Muscle mass will increase your body weight & essentially make you slower or have to work harder on ascents. Your goal with these exercises is to increase muscular strength, endurance & prevent injury. Start with the lower end of the recommended weight for each exercise. As you move through a weight training block be cautious about adding weight to each exercise. Your better off adding reps & maintaining fluidity & form rather than adding weight.
